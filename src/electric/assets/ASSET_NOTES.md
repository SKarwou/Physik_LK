# Bildmaterial

`physik-macht-spass.png` wurde für dieses Projekt mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Werkzeug erzeugt und unverändert in das Repository übernommen. Es handelt sich um motivierende Illustration, nicht um ein physikalisches Schaltbild. Der Spruch wird als zugänglicher HTML-Text dargestellt.

Verwendeter Prompt:

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a motivational cartoon for a German secondary-school physics learning website, to sit beside the chapter title on a warm ivory page (#f4f2eb). Create one square, charming hand-drawn editorial cartoon, suitable for ages 16–19 (witty and warm, not childish). A friendly anthropomorphic light bulb with a warm yellow glowing head, tiny round glasses, expressive curious eyes, fine dark forest-green ink outlines and little arms, looks with delight at a small positively charged red sphere and negatively charged blue sphere on insulated stands on a simple laboratory bench. This is motivational art, not a technical diagram: no electrical wiring, no formulas, no field lines. A few tiny hand-drawn sparkles convey a moment of understanding. Restrained pale sage green and warm cream washes with muted pink/lilac details, lightly textured pencil and ink, generous empty background around the character. Mostly off-white/ivory background, no border. No text, no speech bubble, no lettering, no watermark. Keep all objects comfortably within frame. Appealing, sophisticated cartoon linework with a touch of humor. Use the visual mood of a friendly educational lab mascot. Output image used inside a website.
